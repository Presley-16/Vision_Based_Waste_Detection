Get the PlanetWatch app

Snap illegal dumping, pin it to the map, and help keep your community clean.

Install on Android

Takes about a minute.

  1. Tap Download for Android below.
  2. Open PlanetWatch.apk from your notifications or Downloads.
  3. If asked, allow your browser to install unknown apps, then tap Install.
  4. Open PlanetWatch and sign in — keep Location on.

Add to your iPhone

PlanetWatch runs from your Home Screen, just like an app.

  1. Make sure this page is open in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open PlanetWatch from your Home Screen and allow Location.
