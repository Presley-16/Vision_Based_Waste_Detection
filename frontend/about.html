About Us

Who We Are

The team and mission behind PlanetWatch, and why we're building it for Zimbabwean communities.

PlanetWatch is a vision-based illegal waste dumping detection and reporting platform built for Zimbabwean communities. Citizens capture a photo of a dumping site from their smartphone, our YOLOv11 model automatically classifies the waste, and the report is routed straight to a live map that municipal cleanup teams can act on — turning every phone into a set of eyes for a healthier neighborhood.

Mission

To empower Zimbabwean communities to combat illegal waste dumping through technology, creating cleaner neighbourhoods by connecting citizens directly with municipal authorities for swift waste removal and environmental protection.

Vision

A Zimbabwe free of illegal waste dumping, where every citizen is an environmental guardian and technology bridges the gap between communities and responsive waste management.

Our Values

  • Community empowerment
  • Environmental responsibility
  • Transparency and accountability
  • Innovation for social good
